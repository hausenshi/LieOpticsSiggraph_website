SIGGRAPH ASIA 2026

LieOptics: A Ray-Free Framework for
Optical Modeling and Analysis

  • Haosheng Shi
  • Praneeth Chakravarthula

University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill

Paper

Optical design pipelines, including today’s commercial tools, rely on ray tracing. Ray tracing predicts how well a system images, but not why. It samples rays one at a time and reduces performance to aggregate metrics such as spot diagrams or wavefront error. Aberrations, however, arise from the collective deformation of ray bundles. Sampling individual ray obscures both the structure of that deformation and each element’s contribution. Diagnosing and correcting aberrations remains largely heuristic.

We introduce LieOptics, a framework that models optical elements as analytic Lie-algebraic operators acting directly on ray bundles. Each operator encodes aberration type and order, and the operators compose into a single system-level operator. This exposes the full aberration structure and enables per-element attribution, sensitivity analysis, and principled diagnosis. LieOptics extends ray transfer matrices beyond the paraxial regime to higher-order aberrations, and runs more than 1000× faster than conventional ray tracing.
